Our Story

Building a Better Digital Marketplace for Agriculture

Many farmers still depend on limited local markets and traditional selling methods. Buyers also find it difficult to discover fresh and trusted agricultural products directly from verified sources.

KrisiMarg aims to solve this problem by creating a simple digital marketplace where farmers can list crops, sellers can manage products, and buyers can purchase fresh produce with confidence.

Direct Farmer Access

Buyers can discover products directly from verified farmers and local sellers.

Digital Business Tools

Farmers and sellers can manage products, inventory, orders, and earnings.

Reliable Marketplace Experience

Secure payments, order tracking, customer support, and transparent processes.

Our Mission

Make Agricultural Commerce Simple and Fair

Our mission is to help farmers reach more buyers, improve market access, reduce unnecessary barriers, and build a transparent digital ecosystem for agricultural trade.

Our Vision

A Connected and Empowered Rural Economy

We want to create a future where every farmer can use technology, access wider markets, manage business digitally, and grow with confidence.
