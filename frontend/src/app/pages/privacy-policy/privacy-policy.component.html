By using GaonBazar, you acknowledge that your information may be processed according to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you when you create an account, place an order, register as a farmer or seller, contact support, or use our marketplace services.

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Password or authentication credentials
  • Profile image
  • User role and account status

Farmer and Seller Information

  • Farm or business details
  • Identity verification documents
  • Bank and settlement information
  • Tax or registration information
  • Product and inventory details

Order Information

  • Products purchased
  • Delivery address
  • Order history
  • Payment reference
  • Return and refund requests

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Processing and delivering marketplace orders
  • Verifying farmers, sellers, and transport partners
  • Processing payments, settlements, and refunds
  • Providing customer support
  • Sending order and account notifications
  • Improving marketplace services
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. How We Share Information

We may share necessary information with trusted parties involved in completing marketplace transactions.

  • Farmers and sellers processing an order
  • Delivery and transport partners
  • Payment gateway providers
  • Cloud hosting and technology providers
  • Customer support providers
  • Government authorities when legally required

We do not sell personal information for unauthorized marketing purposes.

4. Payment Information

Online payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Razorpay.

GaonBazar should not directly store complete card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs, or internet banking passwords.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and local storage to:

  • Keep users signed in
  • Remember preferences
  • Maintain cart information
  • Measure website usage
  • Improve website performance

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information.

  • Encrypted network connections
  • Restricted system access
  • Password hashing
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Security monitoring
  • Regular backups

No internet-based system can guarantee complete security.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may request:

  • Access to your personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of eligible information
  • Account closure
  • Changes to communication preferences

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained while your account is active and for additional periods required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

GaonBazar is not intended for children who cannot legally enter into marketplace transactions.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change.
