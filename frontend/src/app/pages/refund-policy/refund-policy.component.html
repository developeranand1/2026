Refund & Return Policy

Understand our guidelines for cancellations, returns, refunds, damaged produce claims, and resolution timelines on KrisiMarg.

At KrisiMarg, customer and farmer satisfaction is our top priority. We provide clear, fair guidelines for returns and refunds on both fresh farm produce and agricultural inputs.

1. Policy Overview

Because KrisiMarg deals with agricultural commodities, perishable fresh produce (like vegetables, fruits, dairy), as well as packaged farming supplies (seeds, fertilizers, tools), return and refund rules are structured based on product category.

2. Order Cancellation Policy

Buyers may cancel orders directly through their dashboard prior to order dispatch:

  • Before Dispatch: 100% full refund with no cancellation charges.
  • After Dispatch (Perishables): Once fresh crops are packed and dispatched, cancellation is generally not permitted unless delivery is substantially delayed past agreed SLAs.
  • After Dispatch (Non-Perishables): Orders can be cancelled prior to arrival, but transport fees may be deducted.

3. Return Eligibility by Category

A. Fresh Farm Produce (Fruits, Vegetables, Grains)

Due to perishable nature, fresh produce returns must be reported within 24 hours of delivery with photographic proof of quality issues or transit damage.

B. Agricultural Equipment & Tools

Eligible for return or replacement within 7 days of delivery if unopened, unused, and in original packaging.

C. Seeds & Fertilizers

Sealed packages are returnable within 5 days if seal is intact and packaging undamaged.

4. Damaged, Defective or Spoiled Produce Claims

If you receive goods that are spoiled, damaged in transit, or mismatched from your order specification:

  1. Take clear photographs/videos of the items and delivery packaging upon receipt.
  2. Go to My Orders > Report Issue on KrisiMarg or contact Support immediately.
  3. Our quality audit team will verify the claim with the farmer/seller within 24-48 hours.
  4. Upon verification, a full or partial refund/replacement will be initiated immediately.

5. Refund Mode & Timelines

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method:

  • UPI / Wallets: 1 - 3 business days
  • Debit / Credit Cards: 5 - 7 business days
  • Net Banking: 3 - 5 business days

6. Non-Refundable Items & Circumstances

  • Perishable items reported after 24 hours of delivery.
  • Products damaged due to improper storage or handling post-delivery by the buyer.
  • Custom bulk harvesting orders specifically requested by buyer once harvesting has commenced.
